# Team4_ENG220_ProjectData

## Abstract

In this project, we will focus on energy usage in Cuba, New Mexico, primarily its solar energy usage. Cuba has a relatively small electrical grid. Within the city, there are no major factories or water filtration centers, and there are also no major retailers located within the town. The closest Walmart is in Española. Due to the small nature of the town and the lack of resources, expanding the grid has proven difficult. Currently, most of the town's houses are powered by solar energy. Cuba has a small solar farm that supplies enough electricity to power roughly 1,000 homes. Without a way to increase power to the town, it will be extremely difficult to build water filtration plants to solve their water crisis. We will do research into how much energy a plant of the needed size will consume, how to increase Cuba's energy supply, and how we can model Cuba's power grid after Taos, which has faced similar issues.
